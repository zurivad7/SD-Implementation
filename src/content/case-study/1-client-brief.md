## The client

Northwind Software is a fictional company. It sells software licenses online to customers across the EU. Customers buy a license, get a key by email, and activate the product on their own computers.

## The problem

Support runs from one shared inbox. Every message lands in the same place, and agents pick what they see first. There are no rules for who handles what, and no targets for how fast a customer gets a reply.

- Customers wait too long for a first reply.
- Enterprise customers, who pay the most, wait in the same queue as everyone else.
- Tickets bounce between agents before the right person picks them up.
- Nobody can say how the team performs, because nothing is measured.

## Goals

The Head of Customer Support set four goals for the rollout:

1. Route every ticket to the right team on arrival.
2. Give each customer tier a clear reply and resolution target, and measure it.
3. Warn a lead before a target is missed, not after.
4. Report the same KPIs every week, so the team can see progress.

## Constraints

- **Time:** 10 weeks from kickoff to the end of hypercare.
- **People:** no new hires. The same 10 agents and 2 leads run the new desk.
- **Service:** support stays open during the change. No downtime at cutover.
- **Data:** customer data stays in the EU and follows GDPR.
- **Budget:** a Zendesk-style service desk on a standard plan. No custom development.

## Stakeholders

| Role | What they need | How involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head of Customer Support (sponsor) | Faster replies, fewer escalations, a weekly KPI report | Signs off each gate |
| Support leads (2) | Clear queues, early warnings, fair workload | Design workshops, UAT, hypercare |
| Support agents (10) | Less sorting, ready-made replies, clear priorities | Interviews, training, UAT |
| Finance | Correct refunds and invoices | Refund and billing process design |
| IT and security | Safe access, GDPR compliance | Access set-up, data review |
| Enterprise account managers | Their customers served first | Enterprise SLA design |
