## The 10-week plan

The rollout ran in five phases, with three formal gates. A gate is a meeting where the sponsor decides, with evidence, whether the project moves on.

[[timeline]]

## Milestones

| Week | Milestone | Evidence for the gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Discovery report shared | Ticket audit, taxonomy, interview themes |
| 4 | **Gate 1: design sign-off** | Groups, routing rules, SLA tiers, macros, signed by the sponsor |
| 7 | **Gate 2: UAT sign-off** | Test scripts passed by agents and leads, open defects reviewed |
| 8 | **Gate 3: go/no-go** | Cutover checklist complete, rollback tested, team trained |
| 8 | Go-live | New desk takes all new tickets |
| 10 | Hypercare closes | Two weeks of stable KPIs, handover to the support leads |

## RACI

R = responsible, A = accountable, C = consulted, I = informed.

| Activity | Head of Support | Project manager | Support leads | Agents | Finance | IT and security |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery and ticket audit | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| Routing and SLA design | A | R | C | C | C | I |
| Desk configuration | I | A | R | I | I | C |
| User acceptance testing | I | A | R | R | C | I |
| Training | I | A | R | C | I | I |
| Go/no-go decision | A | R | C | I | C | C |
| Cutover | I | A | R | C | I | R |
| Hypercare and KPI report | A | R | R | C | I | I |

## Ways of working

- A weekly status report to the sponsor: progress, RAID changes, decisions needed.
- A 15-minute stand-up with the support leads three times a week.
- One RAID log, reviewed every week (chapter 5).
