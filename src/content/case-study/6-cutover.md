## Cutover approach

Cutover happened at the end of week 8, on day 15 of the data. The new desk took every new ticket from the first minute of that day. Tickets already open in the old inbox were finished there by a small team, so no customer had to start again.

## Go-live checklist

| When | Task | Owner role | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 days before | Freeze rule changes | Project manager | Configuration exported and saved |
| 3 days before | Check customer tiers | Enterprise account managers | Tier list signed off |
| 2 days before | Final training and quiz | Support leads | Every agent passed |
| 1 day before | Go/no-go meeting | Head of Support | Decision recorded |
| Go-live, 07:30 | Switch the inbox to forward to the new desk | IT and security | Test email lands in the right group |
| Go-live, 08:00 | First shift starts on the new desk | Support leads | First tickets routed and assigned |
| Go-live, 12:00 | Midday check of routing and SLA timers | Project manager | No unrouted tickets |
| Go-live, 20:00 | End-of-day review | Project manager and leads | Issues logged in the RAID log |

## Rollback plan

We agreed the rollback trigger before go-live, so nobody had to decide under pressure.

- **Trigger:** more than 1 in 10 new tickets routed to the wrong group, or the desk unavailable for more than 1 hour.
- **Decision:** the Head of Support, on advice from the project manager.
- **Steps:** switch inbox forwarding back to the old inbox, pause all triggers, and keep the new desk read-only for the record.
- **Time to roll back:** under 30 minutes. Tested once in week 7.

The rollback trigger was never reached.
