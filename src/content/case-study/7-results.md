## Read this first

**All figures on this page are synthetic.** They come from the demo's generated data, not from a real client. They show how I would measure and report a rollout like this.

## Before and after

The tiles compare tickets created before go-live with tickets created on or after it. Every value is calculated from the data when the page loads.

[[results-tiles]]

## What the numbers say

- **Faster first replies.** Routing sends each ticket straight to a group that can answer it, so less time is lost sorting.
- **Fewer SLA breaches.** The breach rate falls over the first week after go-live, then holds. Early warnings give leads time to act.
- **Fewer hand-offs.** Reassignments drop, because tickets reach the right group first time.
- **More one-touch answers.** Macros let agents solve common requests in a single reply.
- **Customer satisfaction rises a little.** It moves slower than the operational measures, as it usually does.

## How to check them

Open the dashboard and filter by tier, channel or group. The same tiles and charts change together, and the CSV export gives the ticket-level data behind them.
